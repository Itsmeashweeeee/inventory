Daily Work Orders

One card per crew. Add a stop for each jobsite they hit today, in driving order. Give every task a person and a goal like "by lunch" or "6 openings." Anything not finished rolls to the top of that crew's next planned day on its own, with a red day count. Click its X if it's no longer needed. Each crew lead has one permanent link: click "Copy crew link" and send it to him once, by text, email or Phone Link. It always opens that day's plan. Add a plan PDF to a stop to highlight the openings for the crew. Checkmarks show up here as the crew taps tasks done. Everything saves to the CGC Work Orders Google Sheet.

End-of-day reminder

Around 4:00 PM on workdays, the people below get one email listing every task on today's plan that wasn't marked done, with crew notes. Tasks on the list 3 or more days show in red. No email goes out if everything got finished.
